Quantitative Portfolio Research Pipeline

A research-to-production workflow that loads frozen ETF prices into SQL, estimates constrained allocations using Python and pandas, and evaluates them with transaction-cost-aware walk-forward testing.

Research question

Can constraints improve risk-adjusted outcomes?

The experiment tests a six-ETF portfolio covering Canadian equities, US equities, developed international equities, emerging markets, aggregate bonds, and gold.

The strategy maximizes expected Sharpe ratio subject to long-only weights, full investment, and a 30% cap per asset. It is compared with SPY using only information available before each rebalance date.

Research-to-production workflow

A configuration change reruns the complete experiment and regenerates every output.

01

Ingest

Freeze adjusted prices and load normalized observations into SQLite.

02

Analyze

Calculate returns, covariance, correlation, volatility, drawdown, Sharpe, and VaR.

03

Optimize

Solve minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe allocations with explicit constraints.

04

Validate

Walk forward, charge turnover costs, run tests, and publish the research report.

Result

Better risk-adjusted performance, not a manufactured win

SPY produced the higher raw return in this sample. The optimized portfolio produced lower volatility, a smaller maximum drawdown, and a higher Sharpe ratio after modeled transaction costs. The report presents that trade-off directly.

Engineering controls

  • No look-ahead in the rebalance loop
  • Frozen, reproducible market snapshot
  • SQL CTEs, joins, and window functions
  • Weight and chronology tests with pytest
  • MATLAB quadprog implementation included
